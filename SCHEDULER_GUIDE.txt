Additional-function value
-------------------------
Multi-floor Room Availability Map checks every room across a selected stay.
Available rooms show their room number; a room occupied on any selected night
shows X. Prices are intentionally excluded because pricing belongs to Billing.

Double-Booking Conflict Audit compares active bookings in pairs, detects date
range overlaps for the same room and identifies the affected booking IDs.

Console UI layout
-----------------
The Scheduler uses a fixed-width hotel dashboard layout with centred page
headers, role-specific menu descriptions, bordered result panels, occupancy
progress bars, a seven-column monthly calendar, status tags and aligned tables.
Formatting helpers are kept inside scheduler.cpp so other business modules do
not depend on Scheduler presentation details.

Date convention
---------------
A room is occupied from check-in date up to, but not including, check-out date.
For example, a booking from 14/08/2026 to 17/08/2026 occupies 14, 15 and 16.
Customer availability accepts dates from the system date (14/08/2026) through
2028. Employee Daily Schedule and Room Timeline accept 2020 through 2028 so
staff can inspect historical records. Forecast remains current/future only.

Auto-release rule
-----------------
Only an unpaid booking with status PENDING and an expiry date earlier than the
system date is changed to CANCELLED. The employee cannot manipulate the current
date, and historical bookings are retained. The report identifies each released
Booking ID, Customer ID, Room ID, stay dates and expired payment deadline.
